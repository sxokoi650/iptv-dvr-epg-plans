## What does IPTV with DVR actually mean?

Think of the system as three pieces.

**IPTV** delivers the live channels over your internet connection.

**EPG** is the TV schedule. It tells the player what is on, when it starts, and often what the programme is about.

**DVR** is the recording function. It lets you schedule or make a recording so you can watch a programme later.

Those things are related, but they are not the same thing. A service can provide an EPG without providing its own DVR. A player can provide recording while simply using the channel feed and guide supplied by the IPTV provider. Current IPTV guides make the same distinction between provider-side catch-up, player-side recording, and a true cloud DVR.

That is why the phrase **IPTV with DVR** can be a little slippery.

You could have a perfectly usable DVR setup without the provider itself storing your recordings.

### 4. Connection count

Recording can use a stream connection just like normal viewing, depending on the player and setup.

## How much internet speed do you need?

Tivi Live's FAQ recommends at least **16 Mbps for HD** and **25+ Mbps for 4K/UHD**, with a stable wired connection preferred for demanding streaming setups.

For an IPTV with DVR setup, the practical question is not only whether the stream plays.
